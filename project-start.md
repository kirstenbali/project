## Selected project area

Los Angeles crime and public safety analytics. [Community Safety and Resource Planning]

## Why our team is interested in it

Our team is passionate about public safety in Los Angeles. We can connect it to our daily life and continue to grow those connections.

## Candidate Vision A

A resource planning director at the Mayor's Office of Community Safety must decide which LA neighborhoods and times of day should
receive safety resources, such as outreach, late-night services, and lighting.

## Candidate Vision B

A program director at the Mayor's homelessness initiative must decide which areas and services should be prioritized for expanded outreach and victim services for unhoused residents.

## Side-by-side candidate assessment

| Assessment | Vision A | Vision B |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence found so far | The dataset contains a large amount of offense records across all across geographic areas in Los Angeles. They are heavily concentrated in Central, 77th Street, Rampart, Southwest, and Olympic and usually occur during the day. | The dataset identifies whether the victim is homeless or not. It seems that homeless-victim offenses are largely concentrated in Central, Rampart and Newton. |
| Core-data fit | The dataset includes locations, date, times, and offense types, which helps identify patterns in reported offenses. | We have data containing the offenses that are flagged homeless victims, and about majority of the crimes committed in LA are against people who are homeless. |
| Biggest risk | Reported offenses might not be actual offenses. Relying on reported counts could under-report areas since we are not taking into account the crimes that are not reported. | The homeless are the least likely to report crimes, and they are mostly victims in these reported crimes. There must be a low report count in certain areas with high homeless concetration. |
